# BidderFlow

**Turn complex RFPs into proposals your team can approve.**

BidderFlow reads a tender, extracts its requirements, builds the compliance matrix and drafts the technical
response, editable architecture diagrams and effort estimate - in one workspace, with your team reviewing
every step.
